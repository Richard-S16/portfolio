# Portfolio

Richard Schmidt's personal portfolio: the public record of his career and engineering work, aimed at hiring managers, senior engineers and recruiters.

## Language

### Work

**Featured Project**:
A personal, public-repository project chosen to represent Richard's engineering depth, presented with a Case Study.
_Avoid_: Showcase, highlight, side project

**Case Study**:
The written account of one Featured Project: problem, approach, architecture, tradeoffs and status, limited to facts the repository supports.
_Avoid_: Write-up, deep dive, project page

**In-Progress Project**:
A personal project shown publicly while still unfinished, labelled as such and presented without results.
_Avoid_: WIP, beta, upcoming

**Lenovo Project**:
An internal Lenovo R&D product Richard worked on, described only at the level of its public purpose because of confidentiality.
_Avoid_: Client project, work project, professional project

### Career

**Role**:
One job title held at Lenovo R&D for a date range, as written on the Resume and LinkedIn.
_Avoid_: Position, job

**Resume**:
Richard's one-page career document, maintained by him and published unchanged; with LinkedIn, the source for roles, dates, skills and education.
_Avoid_: CV
