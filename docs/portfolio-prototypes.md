# Portfolio homepage prototypes

Mode: Experience. Can a reviewer remember the range of Richard’s frontend work after one screen? Compare with `npm run dev` at `/prototype`. The production homepage and case studies are not part of the experiment.

## Direction contract

- **Thesis:** Show the work as evidence, rather than adding decoration to the existing homepage.
- **Own-world:** Keep Manrope, black, white and red, with only existing project screenshots. Change what gets scale and what a visitor can do first.
- **Story:** Editorial leads with the engineer, Gallery with a project, and Index with the range. All three lead to the real case studies and retain the Lenovo experience and contact sections.
- **First viewport:** Editorial pairs a red nameplate with a two-image spread. Gallery projects ArchLens across a dark stage beside a short introduction. Index puts the introduction in a narrow rail and four linked artifacts in a ruled list.
- **Form:** Compare the cover spread, screening room and specimen register. Surface seed `444bd891`. The code previews are the decision material; none is selected for production.
- **Finish:** Review desktop and mobile, verify that switching and links work, and leave the production design system intact.
