# Lenovo Projects get no Case Study

Lenovo R&D confidentiality forbids exposing internal code, architecture or product details. Lenovo Projects therefore appear only in the Experience section with name, dates, public purpose, stack and at most one homepage thumbnail that Richard supplies and confirms is cleared for public use. Case Study pages (`/work/[slug]`) exist only for Featured Projects from public repositories. Do not "upgrade" a Lenovo Project to a Case Study, or add further screenshots, without written clearance.
